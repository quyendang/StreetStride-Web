Support

Help for routes, permissions, and subscriptions. Email us with the route, device, OS version, and what you were trying to do. That is the fastest way to a fix.

Route coverage

Street View availability varies by location. If a route preview looks sparse, try a shorter route or nearby streets with better coverage.

Location and Health permissions

Location, Health, Photos, and notification access are optional system permissions. Adjust them anytime in your device settings. None of them are required to start a run.

Subscriptions

Purchases and renewals are handled by Apple or Google. Manage or cancel a subscription from your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Run summaries

Photos and share actions happen only when you choose to save or share a summary and grant the related system permission.

Need a hand?

Send a focused note and include screenshots if they help explain the issue.
