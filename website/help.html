Help

Six common tasks, in the order you will actually hit them. For account, billing, or device-specific issues, contact support directly.

Plan a route

Search for places, choose start and destination points, then review the distance and route readiness before you continue to pace.

Start a run

Pick a treadmill pace, confirm the route preview, and begin the countdown when you are ready to move.

Fix route loading

Try nearby roads, a shorter route, or a stronger connection. Some areas have limited Street View coverage and may take a moment to render.

Manage permissions

Use your device settings to change Location, Health, Photos, or notification access at any time. None of them are required to start a run.

Track progress

Open Profile to review streaks, weekly goals, achievements, and saved routes from your local app history.

Share a summary

After a run, use the summary screen to save or share a card, only when you choose to do so.

Still stuck?

Route, device model, OS version, and a short description get you a faster answer.
