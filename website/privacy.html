Privacy policy

StreetStride is a treadmill companion app without a StreetStride account system. This page explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

Overview

StreetStride helps you plan treadmill routes and view Google Street View imagery while you exercise. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

We do not operate a StreetStride account system, and we do not run a StreetStride backend that stores your run history, route history, health data, or precise location data.

Information StreetStride handles

  • Route and location information such as selected start points, destination points, waypoints, route distance, and route progress.
  • Run information such as duration, distance, speed, completion state, achievements, goals, and history saved on your device.
  • Limited app interaction analytics, such as route catalog actions, route source, coverage warnings, and run start or completion events. StreetStride does not send precise route coordinates, place names, health data, or account identifiers in these analytics events.
  • Crash diagnostics used to understand app stability and fix bugs.
  • App preferences such as unit settings, audio choices, theme preferences, and cache controls.
  • Optional photos or share cards only when you choose to save or share them.
  • Optional local reminder settings when you choose to enable workout reminders.

Google and Apple services

StreetStride uses Google Maps Platform services, including maps, directions, place search, and Street View. When you create, search, or preview routes, relevant location, route, device, network, and request information may be sent to Google and handled under Google's terms and privacy policy.

StreetStride uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to measure core route and run interactions and to diagnose crashes. These services are configured without advertising tracking, and StreetStride does not set a user ID.

StreetStride is distributed through the App Store and Google Play, and may use platform services such as in-app purchases, secure credential storage, photos, local notifications, audio, and system permission prompts. Apple's and Google's handling of information is governed by their own privacy practices and your device settings.

On iPhone, if you enable Health integration, StreetStride writes completed treadmill workouts, distance, duration, and estimated active energy to Apple Health. StreetStride does not read your existing Apple Health data.

Your choices and permissions

  • You can manage Location permission in your device settings.
  • On iPhone, you can manage Health permission in the Health app and in Settings.
  • You can manage Photos permission in your device settings.
  • You can manage notification permission and reminder settings in StreetStride and in your device settings.
  • You can delete local app data by removing the app from your device.

Children's privacy

StreetStride is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@streetstride.app.
